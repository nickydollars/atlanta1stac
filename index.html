A New Chapter with
Otto Nemenz International

I'm proud to share with all of my friends and colleagues in the film industry that I am stepping away from the set and into the rental house. After more than twenty years behind the camera and twelve years as a camera assistant — on sets in the New York City and Atlanta market — I am genuinely excited to take on a new role.


I've joined forces with Otto Nemenz International, a legendary Hollywood brand with a hard-earned reputation for reliable, world-class camera service in Los Angeles and Atlanta, and opening soon in New York City. My decision to join Otto came down to alignment — their values, their commitment to the people making films, and the opportunity to serve more of my community than I ever could from the set.


I have taken an honorable withdrawal from Local 600. One day I may be back on set. But for the foreseeable future, I am loving this new role and I'm already connecting talented friends and colleagues with the equipment and service they need to unleash their cinematic vision. Whether you're prepping a feature, a series, or a passion project, I want to be part of your success story.


Come see me. Get in touch. Let's talk cameras.
